## Chapter 12

**8.** *"What remains is the wager: that the same mechanism,
operating now, produces… three or four parallel Tudor settlements"*
(§I). — The sentence folds a DEFEND-tier claim (the mechanism's
present-day operation) inside the wager frame; on the map, only the
casting, ranking and timetable are wagered — the operation itself is
defended.

**9.** *"The United States is the most probable case, and the least
self-aware"* (§IV, opening the candidacies). — A WAGER-tier internal
ranking stated flat and first, and in tension with SPINE §5's cooler
judgment ("has the stack but has not centralised the universalist
platforms, and until it does it is not truly competing"); the
Revisions-3 insertion later in the same section carries the spine's
qualification, but the section still opens on the flat superlative.

**10.** *"China has substantially executed the settlement already"*
(§IV). — The spine HOLDS "China is probably closest to current Tudor
norms" on balance; the draft states it flatter than its rank
("substantially executed," no "probably").

**11.** *"canonically the strongest actor of the four… and
structurally the weakest: no single fisc, no single executive"*
(§IV, the EU assessment). — Substantive divergence from SPINE §5's
"the EU bloc has the tax base and the tools": the draft denies the
single fisc the spine credits; the two judgments agree on the
Depreter conditional but disagree on the EU's fiscal starting
position.
