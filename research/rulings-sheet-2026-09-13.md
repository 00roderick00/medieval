# Rulings sheet — September intake register decisions

## (a) "Price" → establishment/entry/standing cost
DECISION: say establishment cost, entry cost, standing cost — never
unit price; re-grade "no subject could pay" to "no subject could
sustain it as a standing, provincial-scale instrument at campaign
tempo."
DRAFT SENTENCES: ch06 §VII "put the price of decisive force above
what any subject could pay"; ch10 §VII "Prices, however, are facts";
ch06 §VII the census "with one entry."
CARRIERS: Hoffman Tables 2.5–2.6 (pp. 61–63: artillery −1.4%/yr
1382–1439; handguns +3%/yr); Hall p. 58 (powder to a fifth), p. 120
(private siege trains), p. 130 (the administrative threshold);
Thompson in Rogers ed. pp. 279–80 (4–5% budget share; Las Navas);
Vaughan pp. 173, 179 (the 1409 "gunpowder is not to be had"; the
motley train at Rougemont).
RULING: YES (Roderick, 2026-09-16)

## (b) ⚑SPINE "Suppressing" → "subordinating" the intermediaries
DECISION: the chain's last link reads "subordinating," not
"suppressing," every authority between crown and subject; CLAUDE.md
§2's chain and ch10 §I's sentence change with it.
DRAFT SENTENCES: ch10 §I "suppressing every authority between the
crown and the taxable subject"; CLAUDE.md §2 "suppression of
intermediate authorities."
CARRIERS: Tilly p. 25 ("no European state (except, perhaps, Sweden)
made a serious attempt to institute direct rule from top to bottom
until the era of the French Revolution"); chs 10–11's own evidence
(bonds, absorbed orders, licensed retaining, ranked courts).
RULING: "Subordinating." (Roderick, 2026-09-16)

## (c) "Ended" → "locked"
DECISION: the Introduction, ch01 §VI and the Coda say the
overlapping order was LOCKED into units while inter-unit plurality
hardened — not that the plural order was ended in 1440–1500; the
Coda's "ended within two generations" becomes "became irreversible
within two generations."
DRAFT SENTENCES: Introduction §I "ended by an identifiable
mechanism, at an identifiable price, in an identifiable
half-century"; Coda §VI "ended within two generations of that price
becoming payable."
CARRIERS: Strayer pp. 9, 31–34, 57, 91 (the 1300 state; "still not
very strong"; the confessed blank); Scheidel pp. 45, 193, 214, 340,
370–71 (Table 1.1; "consolidation within, plurality between";
four-century rebuild).
RULING: YES (Roderick, 2026-09-16)

## (d) ⚑SPINE §8(e)(6) reworded per Fortescue

and did not see what it bought — replacing "they did not understand
the hidden forces behind the artillery — the fiscal apparatus."

passages built on it.
CARRIERS: Fortescue pp. 114–17 ("the myght of his reaume is
nerehande distroyed therby"; the taille and standing army described

RULING: YES — the defeated saw the fiscal apparatus. (Roderick, 2026-09-16)

## (e) §8(c) oil case: "severs" → "acquires and keeps"
