# Rulings sheet — September intake register decisions

## (a) "Price" → establishment/entry/standing cost
DECISION: say establishment cost, entry cost, standing cost — never
unit price; re-grade "no subject could pay" to "no subject could
sustain it as a standing, provincial-scale instrument at campaign
tempo."
DRAFT SENTENCES: ch06 §VII "put the price of decisive force above
what any subject could pay"; ch10 §VII "Prices, however, are facts";
ch06 §VII the census "with one entry."
CARRIERS: Hoffman Tables 2.5–2.6 (pp. 61–63: artillery −1.4%/yr
1382–1439; handguns +3%/yr); Hall p. 58 (powder to a fifth), p. 120
(private siege trains), p. 130 (the administrative threshold);
Thompson in Rogers ed. pp. 279–80 (4–5% budget share; Las Navas);
Vaughan pp. 173, 179 (the 1409 "gunpowder is not to be had"; the
motley train at Rougemont).
