## What this run is and is not

It applies the rulings to the drafts. It is the first pass since 3 September that edits draft.md, and it edits only what a ruling, a correction of fact, or a re-source-or-cut decision reaches. It is not an expansion pass: a section that is rewritten may grow to what the rewrite needs, and the chapters this run restructures (ch09, ch11, ch12) will grow, but no section is lengthened for length's sake. It is not a new-source pass: nothing enters a draft that is not already in the chapter's sources.md with a pin. The BRIDGE paragraphs remain flagged for Roderick and are not rewritten; where a ruling changes what a bridge must carry (the composite-monarchy disanalogy under (w); the print disanalogy under (p)), write the new bridge as a [BRIDGE — PROPOSED] block beneath the existing one and leave both.

Every draft edit is recorded in the memo under a dated "Phase 5 renovation" Revisions entry: the ruling applied, the sentences changed (old and new), the pins. Memo footers stay PENDING HUMAN REVIEW for the new entries. Commit after each chapter; message names the chapter, the rulings applied, and the word-count change.
