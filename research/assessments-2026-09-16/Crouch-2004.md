## 1. The book in two paragraphs

The 2004 statement is the pamphlet's argument at book length and, sentence for sentence, in the same words. The pole: "while elections certainly exist and can change governments, public electoral debate is a tightly controlled spectacle, managed by rival teams of professionals expert in the techniques of persuasion, and considering a small range of issues selected by those teams. The mass of citizens plays a passive, quiescent, even apathetic part … Behind this spectacle of the electoral game, politics is really shaped in private by interaction between elected governments and elites that overwhelmingly represent business interests" (p. 4). A direction, not a state: "This model, like the maximal ideal, is also an exaggeration … It is my contention that we are increasingly moving towards the post-democratic pole" (p. 4; "towerds" **[OCR]**, corroborated at 2000 PDF 1). The figure: "democracy has moved in a parabola … your pen passes one of the co-ordinates twice: going in towards the centre of the parabola, and then again at a different point on the way out" (p. 5; clean).

The central contention, the sentence ch12 §VII quotes: "while the forms of democracy remain fully in place — and today in some respects are actually strengthened — politics and government are increasingly slipping back into the control of privileged elites in the manner characteristic of pre-democratic times; and … one major consequence of this process is the growing impotence of egalitarian causes" (p. 6; "beck" **[OCR]**, corroborated at 2000 PDF 2). The shell is stated without the word — "Virtually all the formal components of democracy survive within post-democracy" (p. 22; clean) — and the condition defined by what it cannot be called: "One cannot call this kind of politics non- or anti-democratic … At the same time it is difficult to dignify it as democracy itself, because so many citizens have been reduced to the role of manipulated, passive, rare participants" (p. 21; **[OCR]**, corroborated at 2000 PDF 8). The drift is structural: "we must expect an entropy of democracy … Egalitarians cannot reverse the arrival of post-democracy, but we must learn to cope with it" (p. 12; clean).

## 4. What it ADDS

**(1) The ledger check for 2004, question (iii).** Greps across the whole sidecar: **ornament / ornamental / façade / facade / veneer / husk / shell / hollow / decorative / ceremonial — zero, every one.** His words are "spectacle" (p. 4), "forms of democracy" (p. 6), "formal components" (p. 22), "entropy" (p. 12), "parabola" (pp. 5, 20). **The 2004 statement does not change the 2000 verdict: the adjective is free, the noun is his.** The qualification stands in the open: chs. 5 and 6 are not in the file, so the check is complete for what is present and corroborated by the full reading of the 2000 typescript.

**(2) The ellipse — a figure the pamphlet does not develop, and the best available picture of absorption by alliance.** His party is concentric circles (leaders, parliamentarians, activists, members, voters); post-democracy deforms it — "the inner core become stretched from being an inner circle of the party to being an ellipse stretching way out beyond the party's ranks" (p. 73; clean), the far focus being lobbyists and the firms that fund and staff the leadership. The end-state: "a self-reproducing inner elite, remote from its mass movement base, but nested squarely within a number of corporations, which will in turn fund the sub-contracting of opinion-polling, policy-advice and vote-gathering services" (p. 74; clean); the worked case, Forza Italia, "essentially a firm, or network of firms, rather than an organization of the classic party type" (p. 75; clean). For §8(g)(3) the ellipse is the *shape* of the fusion — a leadership whose effective circle lies outside the institution that legitimates it — and more precise than "fusion".

**(3) A qualification he makes against himself, useful to Appendix C.** Forza Italia's purely elliptical form did not hold: "As the years have passed, so it has come to resemble more closely a classic party: it has acquired members and a local voluntary structure, and it has become more successful as a result" (p. 75; clean), local government having reasserted itself as "the prime link between people and politics" (p. 76). A reversal datum from the predecessor's own case, and it belongs beside DC-5.

**(4) For §8(g) item 4 he has no fiscal mechanism — a negative finding worth recording.** The pages present carry no account of how the post-democratic state is financed; the nearest is the exit threat (p. 29). Tilly's mechanism and the book's rent-financing inference are unanticipated by him: his causation runs through class and the firm's mobility, and the fiscal limb is the book's own.

**(5) A caution.** He concedes the optimist's case before answering it — "politicians receive less deference and uncritical respect from the public and mass media than perhaps ever before" (p. 12; clean). A chapter that presents post-democracy as settled fact misreads him; he presents a direction on a scale.

## 5. What it CONTRADICTS

The 2004 book cuts where the pamphlet cut, and the objection stands at full strength in ch12/critiques.md Revisions 15: his firm absorbs the state, not the state the firm, and the parabola has no return stroke. These pages sharpen the first limb — the party itself becomes the firm (pp. 74–75), and state capacity is eroded by sub-contracting "services close to the heart of policy, therefore unable to be fully privatized, and with contracts subject to periodic renewal" (p. 73; clean). **The sharpest versions of the objection — "Government becomes a kind of institutional idiot" and "it is impossible to see any major reversal of them" — lie in chs. 2 and 6 and are NOT in this scan;** critiques Revisions 15 cites them at the 2000 typescript, which remains their only carrier in corpus. The book's answer is unchanged and not restated: the exit option that makes his firm strong does not exist for a foundry, a constellation or a frontier lab whose licence, clearances and revenue depend on one sovereign. Grade unchanged: **good on the disanalogy of object, weaker on predicting the reversal he says is invisible.**
