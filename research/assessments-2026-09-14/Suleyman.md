## 1. The book in two paragraphs

A DeepMind and Inflection co-founder's argument that technology proliferates by law and that this time the law is lethal. "Almost every foundational technology ever invented … follows a single, seemingly immutable law: it gets cheaper and easier to use, and ultimately it proliferates, far and wide" (PDF p. 20 / printed ~6). "From where we stand today, it appears that containing this wave—that is, controlling, curbing, or even stopping it—is not possible" (PDF p. 21 / ~7). Containment is "the overarching ability to control, limit, and, if need be, close down technologies at any stage of their development or deployment" (PDF p. 49 / ~35). Four features — asymmetry, hyper-evolution, omni-use, autonomy — and four incentives — arms race, open science, profit, ego — make refusal impossible: "This is why we won't say no" (PDF p. 161 / ~143).

Part III, "States of Failure", is the part that touches this book. The nation-state is the only possible container ("Nothing else—no other silver bullet—will arrive in time", PDF p. 169 / ~151), yet the wave amplifies its fragility, and the outcome forks: "some liberal democratic states will continue to be eroded from within, becoming a kind of zombie government. Trappings of liberal democracy and the traditional nation-state remain, but functionally they are hollowed out … On another, unthinking adoption of some aspects of the coming wave opens pathways to domineering state control, creating supercharged Leviathans whose power goes beyond even history's most extreme totalitarian governments" (PDF p. 176 / ~158). Hence the dilemma — "sooner or later, a powerful generation of technology leads humanity toward either catastrophic or dystopian outcomes" (PDF p. 25 / ~11) — and ten steps toward a "narrow path" between them. "Containment is not, on the face of it, possible. And yet for all our sakes, containment must be possible" (PDF p. 33 / ~19).

## 2. What it CONFIRMS

**(a) The stack's concentration, stated by a builder.** Ch. 10 rests the decisive layer's capital intensity on SIA/BCG and Bratton; Suleyman states it from inside the industry. "In AI, the lion's share of the most advanced GPUs essential to the latest models are designed by one company, the American firm NVIDIA. Most of its chips are manufactured by one company, TSMC, in Taiwan, the most advanced in just a single building … TSMC's machinery to make these chips comes from a single supplier, the Dutch firm ASML … These three companies have a choke hold on cutting-edge chips … Industrial-scale cloud computing, too, is dominated by six major companies" (PDF pp. 272–73 / ~250–51). His containability checklist is the mechanism as a test: "Silicon chips require specialized and highly concentrated materials, machines, and knowledge … Both help containment in the near term"; "If you can keep price and ease of access out of reach for many, proliferation becomes more difficult" (PDF pp. 254–55 / ~233). The nuclear exception is explained by cost: "Nonproliferation is in no small part a function of the fact that building a nuke is one of the largest, most expensive, and most complicated endeavors a state can embark on" (PDF pp. 56–57 / ~43). Price of the weapon → containability by the few, from a witness with no stake in it.

**(b) The barons hold the cannon.** Ch. 5's platform-as-over-mighty-subject (Bremmer, l. 2495) gains a 2023 confession: "Companies already control the largest clusters of AI processors, the best models, the most advanced quantum computers, and the overwhelming majority of robotics capacity and IP. Unlike with rockets, satellites, and the internet, the frontier of this wave is found in corporations, not in government organizations or academic labs" (PDF p. 207 / ~187); and the East India Company with the right disanalogy — "This process won't, like the East India Company, come enforced at the barrel of a musket, but it will, exactly like the East India Company, create private companies with the scale, reach, and power of governments" (PDF p. 208 / ~188).

**(c) The five moves, as prescription.** Every instrument Chapter 11 predicts appears in step 6 as what must be done. Licensing: "Today anyone can build AI. Anyone can set up a lab. We should instead move to a more licensed environment. This would produce a clearer set of responsibilities and harder mechanisms for revoking access and remedying harms … Just as you cannot simply launch a rocket into space without FAA approval, so tomorrow you shouldn't simply be able to release a state-of-the-art AI" — thresholds "according to model size or capability … training runs of models, chip clusters above a given size" (PDF p. 283 / ~261). In-house state capacity (C2-1): "Ownership gives control" (PDF p. 281 / ~259). Fiscal absorption (C11-1, move 1): "a fixed portion of company value … paid as a public dividend"; "a genuine AGI cannot be privately owned in the same manner as, say, a building or a fleet of trucks" (PDF pp. 284–85 / ~262–63). Add export controls as "one undeniable lever" (PDF p. 272 / ~250), "real censorship, possibly well beyond national borders" (PDF p. 300 / ~278), a ban on open frontier weights (PDF p. 299) and "a bulletproof off switch" (PDF p. 266 / ~244). He does not see that this is the Tudor programme; that is the point of citing him.

**(d) The grip will tighten — his forecast, not only his prescription.** "When the unitary power of the nation-state is threatened, when containment appears increasingly difficult, when lives are on the line, the inevitable reaction will be a tightening of the grip on power" (PDF p. 235 / ~215). Spine §7's "compelled, not accomplished", framed as danger.

**(e) The cannon step, as received wisdom:** "offensive capabilities gave the advantage to those who could afford to buy, build, maintain, move, and deploy capital-intensive cannons" (PDF p. 175 / ~157). Evidence only that the claim is uncontroversial in trade prose — see §3.

## 3. What it CORRECTS

Nothing; no figure, date or attribution in the manuscript is contradicted. The caution runs the other way: his medieval sentences must not enter the provenance chain — "Britain and France" in the Hundred Years' War (PDF p. 175); the stirrup-made-feudalism story told as fact (PDF pp. 202–03 / ~183–84), Lynn White's 1962 thesis, contested for half a century; Urban II banning the crossbow (PDF p. 52 / ~38), an attribution at least disputed. He is a witness to the present, not the fifteenth century.

## 4. What it ADDS

**(1) The containment thesis as a scoreable prediction — Appendix C.** *What he predicts:* (i) "Containment of the coming wave is, I believe, not possible in our current world" (PDF p. 258 / ~236); (ii) the choke points are a closing window — "the next five or so years are absolutely critical, a tight window when certain pressure points can still slow technology down … Eventually, all these technologies will be widely diffused" (PDF p. 273 / ~251; written 2023, so closing c. 2028); (iii) "without a dramatic set of interventions to alter the current course, millions will have access to these capabilities in just a few years" (PDF p. 188 / ~170); (iv) the zombie/Leviathan fork, and corporate challenge — "How these entities are governed, how they will rub against, capture, and reengineer the state, is an open question. That they will challenge it seems certain" (PDF p. 211 / ~191); (v) dated capability claims: human-level performance "within the next three years" (PDF p. 23 / ~9, by 2026); the Modern Turing Test "fully autonomously within three to five years" (PDF p. 90 / ~77); "Within the decade AIs will decide … where military forces are assigned" (PDF p. 222 / ~202, by 2033). *Success on his terms:* "technology whose modes of failure are known, managed, and mitigated, a situation where the means to shape and govern technology escalate in parallel with its capabilities" (PDF p. 253 / ~231), the ten steps as checklist (PDF pp. 297–98). *Failure:* catastrophe (PDF pp. 227–29), dystopia (PDF pp. 235–37), or diffusion past the choke points. *Mapping:* his "containment succeeds" is the book's "state absorbs the stack" — CLAUDE.md §1 claim 3(i); his dystopia is one branch of claim 3(ii) and his zombie government the other — spine §8(g)'s ornamental democracy is the zombie with the Leviathan's apparatus, which "capture, and reengineer the state" nearly names. Two dated rows are worth carrying: **the 2028 choke-point window** — if C10-2's count has risen by then, his diffusion thesis wins and condition 1 moves against the book; if concentration holds past his window, the mechanism is confirmed against a practitioner's expectation; and **the treaty layer** — step 7 ("a UN for AI", a US–China "shared bio-risk observatory", PDF pp. 288–89 / ~266–67) is where the book predicts failure, settlements inside blocs and not across; three years on (export controls, no treaty, WAICO as bloc canon) the record runs the book's way, and C11-2 can say so.

**(2) Ch. 12 §IV and CA-3.** The licensing paragraph (PDF p. 283) as T3 context for CA-3's baseline — an industry call for the instrument spine §5 and §8(c) predict, not evidence of enactment.

**(3) Ch. 5, the interstices.** "Hezbollahization" — "a splintered, tribalized world where everyone has access to the latest technologies" (PDF p. 217 / ~197) — and the diagnosis in his own words: "Something more like the pre-nation-state world emerges in this scenario, neo-medieval, smaller, more local, and constitutionally diverse, a complex, unstable patchwork of polities. Only this time with hugely powerful technology" (PDF p. 221 / ~201). A T3 pairing for the Lebanon exhibit (l. 2362).

**(4) Ch. 12 §VII / Coda, the form question.** Acemoglu and Robinson's corridor: "For every increase in state capacity there needs to be a corresponding increase in social capacity to counterbalance it" (PDF p. 299 / ~277) — spine §8(g)(2)'s falsifiers in another vocabulary; a predecessor to name beside Crouch and Wolin.

**(5) A Scott note.** Totalitarian society "was never fully 'legible' to the state"; "High modernist planners" (PDF p. 211 / ~191) — Scott's vocabulary, uncredited. The book should not follow (Scott assessment §3(i)).

## 5. What it CONTRADICTS

**At full strength.** His central claim about power is the opposite of this book's. "These developments represent a colossal transfer of power away from traditional states and militaries toward anyone with the capacity, and motivation, to deploy these devices" (PDF p. 122 / ~106). "The nature of the four features favors offense: this proliferation of power is just too wide, fast, and open. An algorithm of world-changing significance can be stored on a laptop; soon it won't even require the kind of vast, regulatable infrastructure of the last wave" (PDF p. 187 / ~169). "Maintaining a decisive, indefinite strategic advantage across such a broad spectrum of general-use technologies is simply not possible" (PDF p. 188 / ~170). His Kyiv opening credits thirty hobbyists with turning the battle: "The greatest buildup of conventional military muscle in a generation was humbled … This semi-improvised Ukrainian militia was called Aerorozvidka" (PDF pp. 119–20 / ~103–04). On the state: "New, private interests will step into spaces vacated by overstretched and strained governments" (PDF p. 208 / ~188); "there is no functional 'we' here … Even the president of the United States has remarkably limited powers to alter the course of, say, the internet" (PDF p. 294 / ~272). Read straight: the decisive layer diffuses, offence wins, the state is overtaken — the fragility-amplifier thesis. This reaches the DEFEND-tier claim (spine §6: the mechanism operating now through the AI-military stack) and, in "spaces vacated", spine §8(b)–(c)'s verdict that the American state absorbs the stack.

**The book's best answer.** His own book supplies most of it, because he never reconciles his halves. The diffusion claims concern the *cheap* layer — hobbyist drones, open weights, benchtop synthesisers — and the concentration claims the *decisive* layer — three chip firms, six clouds, models costing "hundreds of millions of dollars to train" (PDF p. 218 / ~198). He calls this "the coming wave of contradictions" and leaves it: "Both will be in play at once" (PDF p. 222 / ~202). The mechanism is the reconciliation — distributed denial below, concentrated decision above (ch. 12 §V) — and he concedes the two facts that answer needs on his own Kyiv page: Starlink "was integral" and conventional aid "undoubtedly had a decisive impact" (PDF p. 120 / ~104); C12-1 scores that dependence. Second, his prescriptions refute his prognosis: the ten steps are the five moves. Third, "the state is overtaken" and "the grip will tighten" cannot both be his forecast; he picks the second (PDF p. 235). Honestly assessed: good on the mechanism and the record to date; exposed at two joints. The five-year window is a dated counter-prediction the book has not yet beaten — 2028 will say. And offence-favours is not answered by patron-dependence if the patrons are themselves the exposed party; the Iran reply (ch. 12 §V) is a claim category, flagged as such, and condition 1 stays live.

**A second contradiction that is an alliance.** "Zombie government … functionally they are hollowed out" (PDF p. 176) and the "shackled Leviathan" (PDF p. 298 / ~276) cut against any reading of claim 3(i) that implies popular control — a reading the spine has already ruled out (§8(g)(1)). Suleyman is a rival to the old claim 3 and an ally of the reworded one: the hollowed democratic form with a consolidated apparatus is his 2023 forecast, and "capture, and reengineer the state" (PDF p. 211) is the fusion §8(g)(3) names as the present indicator.

## 7. Verdict

Suleyman changes neither direction nor register; he adds weight at two places — a practitioner's dated statement that the decisive layer sits in three companies and six clouds, and a ten-step containment agenda that is, item by item, the Tudor programme, written by a man who thinks he is describing its opposite. The single most important thing: his book holds both the book's thesis and its sharpest rival — offence wins, the state is overtaken — never reconciled, and the reconciliation is this book's mechanism; so engage him by name in ch. 12, enter his 2028 diffusion window in Appendix C as a counter-prediction the book must beat, and claim his zombie/Leviathan fork as the 2023 statement of the form question §8(g) has made scoreable.
