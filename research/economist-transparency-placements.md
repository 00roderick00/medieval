# The Economist's transparency essay — placements against the spine

## What the essay gives the book

The essay's central caution — that attributing to weapons the advantages that belong to the systems which use them best has misled military minds before — is the book's sceptics-concede pattern restated by a defence editor in 2026 after eight years of watching the transparency revolution. Historians made the same error about cannon for a century; the artillery state's argument is that the institutions required to own guns, not the guns, did the work. The essay is a mainstream citation for the symmetry: same error, same correction, five hundred years apart.

## Six placements (proposals)

1. **The diffusion objection — capital-intensity migration.** The drone appears to invert the mechanism: $3,000 units on consumer-electronics supply chains, adopted by Hizbullah within months. But the essay locates decisive capacity in the connective tissue — satellite constellations, Maven-class targeting fed by frontier models, kill chains, encryptors procured urgently because data outran networks — all standing, peacetime, continuously funded commitments at bloc scale. Capital intensity migrated from the projectile to the seeing-and-deciding apparatus. The drone is this cycle's arquebus (cheap, diffused, tactically transformative, strategically subordinate); the sensor-targeting complex is the siege train. Diffusion at the tactical layer, consolidation at the systemic layer, simultaneously. Pair with the fifteenth-century handgun/arquebus diffusion material (Hussites; Burgundian ordonnance gunner retainers). Fits whichever chapter answers "modern technology fragments rather than consolidates" — likely ch10 §VIII or ch12; also the modern face of spine §8(a)'s ratchet. Tag as book's-own.

2. **Epic Fury as the living Depreter case.** Depreter's refinement (apparatus without a coherent political centre predicts failure; Burgundy 1477) has a 2026 instance: the most exquisite strike apparatus in history against Iran produced stalemate because the political centre could not specify what the apparatus was for. Necessary, never sufficient. Place beside Burgundy in ch10; it also answers the technological-determinism charge. Flag every Iran BDA figure [RE-CHECK AT PRESS]; CRS R48887 is the primary already in ch12.

3. **ch06 §VI — Talbot and the Belloc syndrome.** Talbot died from underestimating the apparatus; Belloc's Blood (the Maxim-gun couplet) and the knock-out-blow prophets (Freedman's genealogy; Bush 2003; the 2017 "cannot win"; 5,000 targets a day for a war without aims) from overestimating it. Both mistake the weapon for the system. Connects to the Kemp thesis: a targeting pipeline running without defined war aims is artificial authority — machinery without a king. Two-error symmetry as a section device; Freedman as the T2 anchor.

4. **The sceptics-concede arc, compressed.** Biddle ("easy to exaggerate"), Owen (first-rate forces render FPVs irrelevant), Radakin (the Israeli stand-off model) each erode inside the essay — Biddle's own Fort Irwin observation, Gady's Lebanon evidence, Radakin's model tried at scale in Iran. The Rogers/DeVries/Curry arc of thirty years in one article. Candidate for the historiography discussion; also a Current Preoccupations column before it becomes a chapter passage.

5. **The nuclear ceiling — a confessed limit for the conclusion.** The Taiwan material: attacking China's connective tissue means attacking systems that also control nuclear forces; the full transparency war between blocs may be unfightable; nobody knows where the red lines are, only that they exist. The blocs may consolidate but never test each other — a permanent, armed, mutually observed pause rather than a Bosworth. Is that a Tudor settlement or something without precedent? Put the question explicitly in ch12/coda. Consistent with spine §5's nuclear-overhang paragraph and GPT 5.6 Sol's "nuclear as scope condition" (model-council-synthesis.md).

6. **Attrition-belt production as fiscal-mobilisation evidence.** Thousands of FPVs daily, 24,000 UGV medical missions in a quarter, NATO's "failed and failed dramatically" drone exercise, the 1918 who-learns point (doctrine, not tanks, won 1940). Transparency warfare rewards administrative depth and industrial mobilisation — the pressure that forces intermediate polities into blocs. Evidence for the administrative-capacity chapters; the "which polity institutionalises humility" question is an institutional answer of the book's kind.
