## How to score this book

The wager (Introduction, §III) fails if the indicators below move
against it in the directions stated, over the fifteen-year horizon from
publication. Two framing devices precede the numbered indicators.

**The context variable (formerly C1-1): exogenous territorialising
pressure.** Cerny's absent variable, returned. Baseline (2025–26): the
NATO Hague pledge of 24–25 June 2025 ("invest 5% of GDP annually… by
2035"); ReArm Europe's €800bn envelope (4 March 2025); China's 15th
Five-Year Plan self-reliance headline (March 2026); and a great-power
war on the record (CRS R48887, March 2026). Direction: the book expects
this pressure sustained; a lapse to post-Cold-War absence weakens every
indicator below.

**C11-1 — The five-move scorecard (the assembly table).** Each bloc
(US, China, EU, India) scored annually on the five moves: fiscal
absorption of platform surplus; subordination of great subjects
(enforced and upheld); domestication of private force; canonical
assertion (enforced, not merely enacted); communications/legibility
mobilisation. Baseline (mid-2026): the ch11 §VI record — US 4/5 moves
in execution (fiscal absorption partial), China 5/5 in execution, EU
2/5 enforced (canon enacted but delayed; no fisc), India 2/5
(legibility and fiscal; military-industrial lagging). Direction: the
wager's condition 4 fails the book if the scorecard stalls across all
four candidates.

## The indicators

**C10-2 — Leading-node concentration ratio** *(absorbs C6-1)*.
Measures: polities/jurisdictions able independently to field the
decisive layer (leading-node fabrication; proliferated military LEO;
hyperscale sovereign compute; peer-war munitions base). Baseline: 100%
of sub-10nm capacity in Taiwan (92%) and South Korea (8%) (SIA/BCG,
April 2021); one EUV supplier on earth, none shipped to China; two
polities flying proliferated military-relevant LEO (mid-2026).
Direction: count holds at ≤4 → thesis holds; rises materially above
four → wager condition 1 fails the book.

**C10-1 — Public financing of the military-relevant stack** *(absorbs
C6-2)*. Measures: central-budget lines for the stack, and their form
(subsidy → equity → ownership). Baseline: CHIPS $52.7bn appropriated
(Pub. L. 117-167, 9 Aug 2022) with the $8.9bn grant-to-equity
conversion (~10% of Intel, 22 Aug 2025); Big Fund III RMB 344bn (May
2024); EU Chips Act with ~€4.5bn directly Commission-controlled and
Magdeburg cancelled (July 2025). Direction: rising public share and
escalating form (toward equity/ownership) → thesis holds.

**C10-3 — Decisive-layer delivery gap** *(incorporates C5-1)*.
Measures: stated capability targets vs audited actuals. Baseline:
155mm at ~36k/month actual (DoD IG, March 2026) against the 100k
goal; average major-system fielding >12 years, lead frigate 3+ years
late (GAO); NDIS (Jan 2024): the base "does not possess the
capacity… required." Direction: a closing gap → consolidation
executing; a chronically open gap → the artillery-state response
failing in the probable case — scored against the US candidacy, not
the mechanism.

**C12-1 — Patron-dependence of the distributed layer** *(replaces
C6-4)*. Measures: whether cheap distributed systems operate without a
patron's intelligence, connectivity and interceptor layers. Baseline:
the March 2025 suspension week (aid 3rd; intelligence ~5th; imagery
8th; restoration 11th); ~42,000 Starlink terminals as the backbone;
Patriot stock at ~25% of planning requirement (July 2025). Direction:
a full-stack power defeated by unpatroned distributed systems fails
wager condition 1.

**C12-2 — Industrialisation of the cheap layer.** Measures: whether
the distributed layer's scaling re-concentrates into state industrial
policy. Baseline: Ukraine 2.2M drones produced 2024 (Zelensky, Feb
2025), ~3M FPVs delivered 2025 (Shmyhal, Dec 2025); Russian Shahed
launches from ~200/week to >1,000/week by March 2025 (CSIS); the
805-drone night of 6–7 Sept 2025 (Ukrainian Air Force). Direction:
continued industrialisation → the "cheap" layer confirms, rather than
refutes, the fiscal-military pattern.

**C2-1 — Nationalisation-of-function index** *(pairs with C4-1)*.
Measures: state absorption of platform functions (identity, payments,
compute, orbital, model provision) into public or publicly-controlled
instruments. Baseline (2025–26): Starshield ("owned and controlled by
the US government"); the GSA $1 agency AI deals (Aug 2025); e-CNY
operations centre; WAICO signed by 29 states (16 July 2026); the
Private Economy Promotion Law sequence (May 2025). Direction: rising
→ the Henrician settlement executing.

**C4-1 — Absorption-mode index.** Measures: *how* each bloc absorbs —
procurement/personnel vs regulation vs equity vs fusion. Baseline: US
procurement-and-personnel (programmes of record; Detachment 201, 13
June 2025; verified absence of golden shares in Palantir/Anduril —
against the Intel equity exception); EU canonical; China fused; India
sovereign-stack. Direction: convergence toward harder forms (equity,
fusion) → acceleration; scored per bloc.

**C2-3 — Platform-independence counter-indicator** *(the falsifier;
pairs with C3-2)*. Measures: an actor combining decisive-relevant
capability with a majority-commercial fiscal base that escapes state
instruments *and resolves disputes with states in fora states do not
control*. Baseline: zero instances — the strongest candidate episode
(Anthropic, 2026) was resolved in an Article III court, the state's
own forum, both parties bound (injunction 26 Mar 2026; restoration 3
Apr 2026). Direction: a genuine instance fails wager condition 3.

**C3-2 — Independent-fiscal-base emergence (the condottiere watch)**
*(refined by C4-2; context from C3-1)*. Measures: contractor-class
actors' revenue independence from the state paymaster. Baseline: the
conventional tier files 87–98% government revenue as a formal risk
(Booz Allen 98%, CACI 95.7%, Leidos ~87%, FY2023–25 10-Ks); the
frontier labs invert it (state ceilings ~$200M vs reported
multi-billion commercial run-rates, 2025–26, estimates flagged; a
$65bn raise during a federal blacklist, reported). Direction: the
inversion spreading beyond the labs, or a lab converting revenue into
recognised standing, moves condition 3's precursors.

**C7-1 — Populist-absorption index.** Measures: whether
propertied-anxious waves durably reduce the state's
fiscal-administrative reach (the ch07 falsifier). Baseline: the
post-Brexit UK tax take projected toward "a post-war high of 37.1% of
GDP by 2028-29" (OBR, March 2024); no gathered instance of durable
reach-reduction. Direction: a populist wave that durably shrinks
fiscal reach counts against the book.

**C8-2 — State reassertion over private fora** *(absorbs C8-1)*.
Measures: the tempo of state subordination of private/platform
adjudication. Baseline: Achmea (2018)/Komstroy (2021); ECT withdrawal
effective 27 June 2025; DSA Art. 21's non-binding cap; MiCA fully
applicable (Dec 2024); Binance $4,316,126,163 (21 Nov 2023); Google
remedies (Sept 2025). Direction: continued subordination-not-abolition
→ hierarchical plurality consolidating.

**C8-3 — Grey-zone legibility** *(absorbs C1-3)*. Measures: the
offshore stock and, decisively, its untaxed fraction. Baseline:
offshore household financial wealth ≈10% of world GDP; untaxed
fraction ~25%, down by a factor of ~3 since 2013; $12.6tn
CRS-reported in 2022 (EU Tax Observatory 2024). Direction: untaxed
fraction falling → legibility winning; rising again → wager condition
2 moves against the book.

**C11-2 — Bloc-internal vs cross-bloc fiscal reach.** Measures: the
divergence between within-bloc consolidation and between-bloc
harmonisation. Baseline: CRS/AEOI at 116 jurisdictions, 171M
accounts, ~€13tn (2024; OECD 2025) — against the Side-by-Side
carve-out taking US-parented groups outside the IIR/UTPR (applying 1
Jan 2026) and Canada's DST rescission (June 2025). Direction:
bloc-internal reach rising while cross-bloc instruments fragment →
the parallel-settlements geometry; bloc-internal reach *falling* →
condition 2 against the book.

**C11-3 — Court-check series.** Measures: instances of consolidating
states losing against their own subjects in their own courts — the
settlement's rule-of-law form, kept scoreable. Baseline (2025–26):
FTC v. Meta lost (18 Nov 2025, appealed); Apple contempt affirmed
against Apple (11 Dec 2025); GC upholds gatekeeper designation
against Apple (8 July 2026); the Anthropic injunction against the
state (26 Mar 2026). Direction: a continuing mixed series →
discipline-through-process (the book's reading); systematic state
defeat on the *core* moves → the settlement failing.

**C6-3 — Capitulation-as-procurement.** Measures: polities choosing
to buy/license/align rather than build the decisive layer — ch06's
surrender-without-siege as modern procurement. Baseline: two polities
field proliferated military LEO; the EU contracted to ~2030 (IRIS²,
16 Dec 2024); client-state patterns in chip and model licensing
(2025–26). Direction: alignments deepening → the cascade operating;
independent builds proliferating → scored with C10-2 against the
book.
