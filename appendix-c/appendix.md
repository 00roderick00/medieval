## I. How to score this book

The wager is the Introduction's, in the four numbered conditions of its
§III and over its fifteen-year horizon from publication; neither is
restated here. This appendix supplies the instruments those conditions
name, and it adds the second set the reworded third claim requires. The
book now predicts two things, scored separately (CLAUDE.md §1, claim 3,
as reworded 16 September 2026; spine §8(g)): first, that the independent
technological powers are subordinated to the state — the mechanism, and
the subject of Parts II and III below; second, that the constitutional
form of the state which absorbs them is open, and that in the democratic
bloc it may prove ornamentally democratic — legitimating authority
demonstrably separated from effective control of the state's strategic
commitments — as well as democratic. The second is the subject of Part IV.
The barons' loss of independence and the people's acquisition of control
are not assumed to be the same event, and the reader who finds the first
confirmed and the second failed should record both.

Seven scoring rules govern every row that follows.

**1. Four blocs, scored separately.** The candidate consolidators are the
United States, China, Europe and — conditionally — India (spine §5). Each
indicator carries a baseline for each bloc where one is sourced, at the
level stated, and the words NO BASELINE — not scored where none is. No
bloc's score is inferred from another's. The Indian column is empty
throughout: none of the works verified for this appendix to 16 September
2026 supplies a dated Indian baseline, and the figures the chapter record
holds for India (the Aadhaar count, the monthly UPI volume, the IndiaAI
compute tallies) are unverified web pointers on ch11's retrieval list,
which this appendix may not carry. India therefore enters the frame as a
named bloc whose rows await their baselines; the earlier assembly's
"India 2/5" cell is withdrawn to the memo's candidate list for the same
reason.

**2. Four levels, distinguished.** Every indicator is read at four levels
and the level at which its baseline sits is stated: *enacted authority*
(a statute, regulation, decision or pledge with a date — the state has
claimed the power); *demonstrated compliance* (the subject has done what
the authority requires, or has been made to — a designation accepted, a
fine paid, a divestiture executed, a licence taken); *operating capacity*
(the state, or the bloc, can itself do the thing — build, field, finance,
replace); and *democratic accountability* (the authority was granted, is
conditioned, and can be withdrawn by the bloc's representative
institutions — the level Part IV scores). A baseline at one level is not
evidence at another; an authority enacted is not a capacity, and a
capacity is not an accountability. Where a row has a baseline at one level
only, the others are printed NEEDS BASELINE and not inferred. For the
Chinese bloc the fourth level is null by construction and is printed n/a,
not zero; Chinese rows are scored on three levels.

**3. The American rule.** A failed American prediction is recorded as a
failure of the wager regardless of the score elsewhere. The book's verdict
on the United States is declared (spine §8(b), (c): the state will absorb
the stack), and its reading of the American case as the latecomer's
position rather than a counterexample (spine §7) is a claim that cannot be
rescued by a Chinese or European result. The rule applies to the American
column of every indicator in Parts II–IV and to the whole of Part V.

**4. The re-dating rule.** Every indicator is dated and directional when
entered and none is re-dated or re-pointed after the fact; a threshold
that is missed is scored as missed. The apparatus is built not to be the
comet of 1456, which Chastellain hedged in advance ("posé que elles ne
soient approuvées, ne tenues à nécessaires", *Œuvres* t. III, p. 117)
and cashed afterwards on whichever princes died (p. 387); the
astrologers' fixed term for Charles VII, who "ne passeroit point le mois
de mars" and lived (pp. 444, 446), is the honest form of a forecast, and
it is the form this appendix takes. Tilly's precedent stands beside it:
theory "must proceed in an open-ended and prospective fashion" (Tilly
1975, p. 635).

**5. Three coding rules for the absorption rows.** (a) A stalled move is
classed before it is scored: a failure of interest or will is
distinguished from a failure of power (Goldsmith and Wu 2006, p. 89), and
only incapacity counts against the mechanism; a will-classed stall is
scored against the American route's timetable, which under rule 3 is
enough. (b) Every American absorption event is coded as *coercion*
(licence, off-switch, structural separation) or *contract* (the barons
invited in — a revenue share, an equity stake taken by agreement, a
commission granted); Krasner's regularity that strong states lose
autonomy only by invitation (Krasner 1999, p. 29) is the rule's warrant,
and the contract side is the present indicator of the ornamental route
(spine §8(g) item 3; DC-5 below). (c) Consolidation of a platform with the
state as client rather than sovereign — services rendered, immunity
granted, a merger approved — is recorded as a failure on the American
route, not as consolidation; and a licence counts as consolidation only
where revocation has been exercised or is exercisable against an
incumbent, not where it freezes rivals in the incumbent's favour (the
capture-by-licence reading is Wu's, *The Master Switch*, ch. 9, and it is
scored against the book, not for it).

**6. Democracy and liberalism, scored separately.** The distinction is
Crouch's — a vigorous liberal society "is not the same as a strong
democracy" (Crouch 2000, PDF pp. 5–6) — and it governs Part IV: open courts,
free speech and a mixed litigation record are evidence of liberal
vitality and are scored on C11-3 and C8-2; they are not evidence that
elections, legislatures or public institutions control the strategic
commitments, which DC-1..4 score. A bloc may pass the liberal test and
fail the democratic one, and the appendix is built to show it.

**7. Baseline notation.** DATED — a figure or an enacted text with a date
and a source at the tier stated. NEEDS VERIFICATION — a dated figure whose
primary instrument is on retrieval or whose pin is owed; carried, not
scored, until the pin lands. NEEDS BASELINE — a measure the row requires
and no source yet supplies. NO BASELINE — not scored — a bloc for which
the row has nothing. The primary instruments opened at the sidecar for
this rebuild are: Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 (eIDAS 2.0), OJ L, 30 April
2024; COM(2024) 150 final (the European Defence Industry Programme
proposal, 5 March 2024 — a proposal, not a regulation); the National
Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2026, S. 1071, 119th Congress,
enrolled text (the enactment date is not in the sidecar and is pinned at
press); the Hague Summit Declaration, 25 June 2025; the CHIPS and Science
Act, Pub. L. 117-167 (9 August 2022); the GENIUS Act, Pub. L. 119-27
(18 July 2025); Regulation (EU) 2022/1925 (DMA); Regulation (EU)
2022/2065 (DSA); Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act); S. 1671, 118th
Congress (the Digital Platform Commission Act of 2023, as introduced);
the DOJ press release 22-1222 (19 October 2022); California SB 53
(Chapter 138, Statutes of 2025); the BIS final rule at 90 FR, doc.
2025-16735 (2 September 2025); and the CMA cloud services final decision
(31 July 2025). Figures from the chapter record are cited to the chapter
sources.md that holds them.

## II. The context variable and the scorecard

**CV — Exogenous territorialising pressure** *(formerly C1-1)*. Cerny's
absent variable, returned; not a falsifier but the condition under which
every row below is read. *Level:* enacted authority (pledges and plans).
*United States and Europe:* the Hague Summit Declaration, para. 2 —
"Allies commit to invest 5% of GDP annually on core defence requirements
as well as defence-and security-related spending by 2035" — and para. 3,
"at least 3.5% of GDP annually" on the NATO definition, "up to 1.5%" on
infrastructure, networks, resilience and the industrial base, with "annual
plans showing a credible, incremental path" and a review "in 2029" (25
June 2025; verbatim at the sidecar; DATED). Europe additionally: ReArm
Europe / Readiness 2030, 4 March 2025, up to €800bn (Commission; ch12
sources.md; DATED). *China:* the 15th Five-Year Plan's "greater
self-reliance and strength in science and technology" (gov.cn, 13 March
2026; ch12 sources.md; DATED). *India:* NO BASELINE — not scored. A
great-power war is on the record for the period (CRS R48887, March 2026;
ch12 sources.md). *Direction:* the book expects the pressure sustained; a
lapse toward the post-Cold-War absence — the Hague trajectory abandoned at
the 2029 review, the plan's self-reliance line dropped — weakens every
indicator below. The dated priors it must beat are in Part VI.

**C11-1 — The five-move scorecard.** Each bloc scored annually on the five
moves of ch11 §VI: fiscal absorption of platform surplus; subordination
of great subjects, enforced and upheld; domestication of private force;
canonical assertion, enforced and not merely enacted; communications and
legibility mobilisation. Each cell is coded by *target* (own subject or
peer state) and by *status* (asserted, enforced, enforced-and-upheld) —
which is to say at the first two levels — with the third and fourth
levels carried by the rows in Parts III and IV. *United States:* four of
five moves in execution at the enacted level, fiscal absorption partial
(ch11 §VI record; the instruments are itemised at C10-1, C2-1/C4-1, C8-2
and Part V; DATED). *China:* the earlier "5/5 in execution" is
re-expressed layer by layer, on Cheung 2022: executed at canon and
legibility; inherited at the defence stack, which the state never lost to
private hands; unexecuted at the seam between them, where "approximately
three thousand firms" in a civilian technology sector of "more than
130,000" give "a participation rate of just 2.3 percent" (p. 116) and the
state's own conglomerates keep an "iron grip on licenses" (p. 115); and
dependent at the chokepoint. The Chinese canon row starts not in 2020 but
in 2002, with the "Public Pledge on Self-Discipline for the Chinese
Internet Industry" (Goldsmith and Wu 2006, pp. 9, 96), so that 2020–23 is
scored as discipline of domestic subjects only. Every Chinese cell from
2019 carries Cheung's warning that "public reporting on the CMCFDC's
activities stopped beginning in 2019" (p. 102): scored against
concealment, and printed so. (DATED, 2017–18; democratic accountability
n/a.) *Europe:* canon enacted — the DMA applicable from 2 May 2023 (Art.
54), the DSA, the AI Act applicable from 2 August 2026 with prohibitions
from 2 February 2025 and general-purpose model obligations from 2 August
2025 (Art. 113), and eIDAS 2.0 (Art. 5a) — with the first enforcement
decisions taken (C8-2); no fisc — the Union budget "merely 1.27 per cent"
of combined GDP (Zielonka 2006, p. 96; the fiscal zero), and the
fiscal-military instrument at proposal stage (C10-1; EDIP). *India:* NO
BASELINE — not scored (the "2/5" of the first assembly withdrawn; rule 1).
*Direction:* rising enforced-and-upheld actions against own subjects
inside each bloc is the Tudor reading; decisive actions clustering
bloc-against-bloc while internal enforcement stalls is the great-power
reading; condition 4 fails the book if the scorecard stalls across all
four candidates, subject to rule 5(a) and, for the United States, rule 3.
Two named failure modes are scored here: the reconstitution outcome (a
severance reversed with the state's approval — Wu, ch. 18) and the 1695
outcome (a licence that lapses because the licensor cannot run the
medium — ch11).

## III. The indicators — claim 3(i), the mechanism

**C10-2 — Leading-node concentration ratio** *(absorbs C6-1)*. Measures
the number of polities able independently to field the decisive layer:
leading-node fabrication, proliferated military low-orbit constellations,
hyperscale sovereign compute, a peer-war munitions base. *Level:*
operating capacity. *United States:* the 2021 snapshot places 100 per cent
of sub-10nm capacity in "Taiwan (92%) and South Korea (8%)" (SIA/BCG,
April 2021; ch10 sources.md), neither a bloc core; the erosion of that
figure by the CHIPS-financed fabs is the American series; one EUV supplier
on earth and no system shipped to China (ch10 sources.md); the Space
Development Agency's Tranche 3 award (~$3.5bn, December 2025; ch10
sources.md) and the NDAA FY2026's extension of the middle-tier authority
to Tranches 4–6 (Sec. 1604). DATED. *China:* 24.25 per cent of the BIS
Entity List — 454 of 1,872 entries — by end-March 2021 (Cheung 2022, p.
282); the SMIC 7nm teardown (ch10 sources.md); the Qianfan and Guowang
constellation counts (mid-2026; state-media caveat; NEEDS VERIFICATION).
*Europe:* the enabling supplier is European and the leading node is not —
ESMC Dresden's €5bn aid (Commission IP/24/4287, 20 August 2024) and the
Magdeburg cancellation (July 2025; Intel Q2 2025 release as T1 anchor, on
retrieval — NEEDS VERIFICATION); IRIS² contracted, services c. 2030 (ch10
sources.md). DATED at the supplier level. *India:* NO BASELINE — not
scored. *Direction:* a count holding at four or fewer confirms the
concentration thesis; a rise materially above four fails condition 1. The
foundry's pricing power is scored apart from the contractor class's
subordination (C3-2) and the two are not merged: McNeill's Liège held its
price for two centuries after the barons were commissioned ("Even the
mightiest rulers had to pay what was asked, or do without", McNeill 1982,
p. 113); the pricing series itself NEEDS BASELINE.

**C10-1 — Public financing of the military-relevant stack** *(absorbs
C6-2)*. Measures central-budget lines for the decisive layer and their
form, from subsidy through equity to ownership. *Level:* enacted
authority (the appropriation); operating capacity (the disbursement).
*United States:* the CHIPS and Science Act appropriates to the CHIPS for
America Fund $24.0bn (FY2022), $7.0bn (FY2023), $6.3bn (FY2024), $6.1bn
(FY2025) and $6.6bn (FY2026) — $50.0bn, of which $39.0bn for the §9902
incentives (Pub. L. 117-167, Sec. 102(a)(2); verified at the statute; the
$52.7bn headline of ch10 sources.md adds the Act's defence, international
and workforce funds); the awards of November–December 2024 (Intel
$7.86bn, TSMC $6.6bn, Samsung $4.745bn, Micron $6.165bn; Commerce/NIST
releases; ch10 sources.md); the $8.9bn grant-to-equity conversion, ~10 per
cent of Intel, August 2025 (Intel newsroom; ch10 sources.md) — the
paymaster becoming owner, the one such event in the American record, and
coded *contract-ownership* under rule 5(b); the NDAA FY2026's Defense
Industrial Base Fund extended to microelectronics, unmanned and
attritable vehicles, defence space systems and batteries (Sec. 867, new
10 U.S.C. §4817(g)) and its multiyear procurement authority for named
munitions "beginning in fiscal year 2026" (Sec. 804(b)). DATED. *China:*
Big Fund I–III at RMB 138.7bn, 204bn and 344bn (III, May 2024; ch10
sources.md — the Bloomberg canonical is bot-blocked; NEEDS VERIFICATION
at the primary); the state's stack financed also from securitised
household savings — RMB 2.548tn of defence-conglomerate asset
securitisation in 2012–20 and eighty-three MCF guidance funds with RMB
872.3bn raised by end-2019 against RMB 1.583tn targeted, together "the
equivalent of 41 percent of the average official annual defense budget"
(Cheung 2022, pp. 123, 127, 278; DATED 2019–20); the techno-security state
at "between 3.4 percent and 3.6 percent of GDP" against the American

at €4.175bn and the Commission's directly controlled share of the €43bn
headline at ~€4.5bn (Bruegel's decomposition — T3; ch10 sources.md);

about 60 per cent public (ch10 sources.md); and the European Defence
Industry Programme, whose proposal sets a "financial envelope … to
31/12/2027" of "EUR 1 500 million in current prices" (COM(2024) 150
final, 5 March 2024, §4) — a PROPOSAL, entered as such; the adopted
regulation, if and when adopted, is this row's first European movement
at the enacted level. DATED at proposal. *India:* NO BASELINE — not
scored. *Direction:* a rising public share and an escalating form,
towards equity and ownership, confirms the mechanism; a shift of the
military-relevant layer to distributed private financing counts against
it. The fiscal *mode* — rents or bargained taxation — is scored
separately at DC-6.

**C10-3 — Decisive-layer delivery gap** *(incorporates C5-1)*. Measures a
bloc's stated capability targets against audited actuals. *Level:*
operating capacity. *United States:* 155mm production at ~36,000 rounds a
month (DoD Inspector General, as of March 2026; relayed at T3, the IG

sources.md; DATED); the National Defense Industrial Strategy's own
confession that the base "does not possess the capacity … required"
(January 2024; page pin owed); and the next datum already ordered — the
NDAA FY2026's report on critical munitions for simultaneous conflicts,
due 180 days after enactment, with "an estimate of the number of days …

rates needed to close the shortfall (Sec. 364(a)(2)(B), (D)). *China:*
the PLA centennial target of 2027 (Cheung 2022, p. 308) is a stated target
without an audited actual; NO BASELINE at the audited level — not scored.
*Europe:* ASAP's "2 million shells per year" is a capacity claim, not an
output (Commission IP/24/1495); Magdeburg cancelled; IRIS² services c.
2030. DATED at the claim level; the audited series NEEDS BASELINE.
*India:* NO BASELINE — not scored. *Direction:* a closing gap is the
response executing; a chronically open gap is hollow sovereignty in the
decisive domain — scored, for the United States, against the American
candidacy under rule 3.

**C12-1 — Patron-dependence of the distributed layer** *(replaces
C6-4)*. Measures whether cheap distributed systems operate without a
patron's intelligence, connectivity and interceptor layers; the bloc
scored is the patron. *Level:* operating capacity (the patron's); enacted

BASELINE as patron — not scored. *Direction:* a full-stack power defeated
by unpatroned distributed systems fails condition 1; persistent patron
dependence confirms concentration.

**C12-2 — Industrialisation of the cheap layer.** Measures whether the

scored. *Direction:* continued industrialisation confirms that the cheap
layer behaves as fiscal-military capacity; a decisive employment of it at
non-state scale, without a patron, cuts against the book at C12-1.

*India:* NO BASELINE — not scored. *Direction:* rising absorption, and
convergence towards the harder instruments, is the Henrician settlement
executing; each bloc deepening its own mode is the parallel-settlements

**C2-3 — Platform-independence counter-indicator** *(with C3-2, the
condottiere watch; the falsifier of condition 3)*. Measures an actor
combining decisive-relevant capability with a majority-commercial fiscal
base that escapes state instruments *and resolves its disputes with
states in fora states do not control*; C3-2 supplies the precursor series

*Level:* demonstrated compliance. *United States:* zero instances. The
strongest candidate episode — the GSA's order of 27 February 2026 to
"IMMEDIATELY CEASE all use of Anthropic's technology", the preliminary
injunction of 26 March 2026 (N.D. Cal. 26-cv-01996-RFL) and the GSA's
restoration of 3 April 2026 "to the status quo in effect prior to
February 27, 2026" — was resolved in an Article III court, the state's
own forum, with both parties bound (ch11 sources.md; DATED). The
precursor series: the conventional tier files its dependence as a risk —
Booz Allen 98 per cent, CACI 95.7 per cent, Leidos about 87 per cent of
revenue from the United States government (SEC 10-Ks, FY2024–25; ch03
sources.md; DATED) — and the frontier laboratories invert it, state
ceilings of about $200m (CDAO release; pin owed) against reported
multi-billion commercial run-rates (estimates; NEEDS VERIFICATION). The
inversion is dated to its origin: the Department of Defense "accounts for
a mere 3.6 percent of global R&D outlays compared to 36 percent at its
height in 1960" (Cheung 2022, p. 290). *China:* no private actor passes
the conjunction; the unsubordinated subjects are the state's own
monopolies (Cheung 2022, p. 115; DATED 2017–18). *Europe:* no instance
sourced; the Mistral–ASML holding is a T4 pointer — NEEDS VERIFICATION.
*India:* NO BASELINE — not scored. *Direction:* a genuine instance fails
condition 3; the inversion spreading beyond the laboratories, or a
laboratory converting revenue into recognised standing, moves the
precursors.

## IV. The democratic-control sub-wager — claim 3(ii), the form

The claim is that the state which absorbs the stack may be governed, in
the democratic bloc, by institutions that legitimate its strategic
commitments without controlling them. "Ornamental" is a demonstrable
separation, not a synonym for bureaucracy, unpopular decisions or
imperfect democracy (spine §8(g) item 2), and it is a special case of the
condition Crouch named: "while the forms of democracy remain fully in
place — and today in some respects are actually strengthened — politics
and government are increasingly slipping back into the control of
privileged elites" (Crouch 2000, PDF p. 2), restricted here to the strategic
commitments that constitute the decisive stack and given falsifiers. The
principle the falsifiers operationalise is his too: democracy "has
depended on the capacity of politicians to reduce the political power of
the military while at the same time sustaining its effectiveness as a
fighting force" (PDF p. 32). Three design rules come from Bagehot, credited
for the distinction and not for his valuation of it. Every falsifier
measures desuetude, not abolition — the danger "is not in assassination,
but atrophy; not abolition, but decline" (Bagehot 1867/72, Gutenberg PDF p. 58) — so an
institution that survives and does not act is scored as ornamental. Only
remedies count, never punishment: a change of Ministry "may not be a
remedy at all; it may be only a punishment. A Minister may risk his
dismissal; he may do some act difficult to undo, and then all which may
be left will be to remove and censure him" (Introduction to the 2nd edn, 1872; PDF p. 11)
— so DC-1 and DC-3 count only what undid or redirected a commitment. And
a manufactured assembly requires a centralised executive — the Second
Empire's chambers, where "a few permitted objectors fill the air with
eloquence, which every one knows to be often true, and always vain" (PDF
p. 71) — so the legibility rows (C8-3, C11-2, eIDAS 2.0) are read here as
inputs to the ornamental route and not only as consolidation signals.
The Chinese column is n/a throughout Part IV by construction, except at
DC-4 and DC-6, where China supplies the inverse case.

The four falsifiers are the wager's; if the democratic bloc shows all
four operating on the stack's commitments across the horizon, claim
3(ii)'s ornamental outcome is falsified for that bloc and the settlement
is scored democratic. Each is entered with its baseline as a figure or an
enacted text with a date, or marked NEEDS BASELINE and not scored.

**DC-1 — Elections that redirect strategic commitments.** Measures
whether an election turning on a stack commitment reversed or redirected
it. *Level:* democratic accountability (the row's own); the baseline sits
at enacted authority. *United States and Europe:* the one dated
commitment whose electoral survival can be scored from 2026 is the Hague
pledge — 5 per cent of GDP by 2035, 3.5 per cent on the NATO definition,
annual plans, review in 2029 (Hague Summit Declaration, paras 2–3; DATED)
— scored on whether any national election between 2026 and 2035 that
turns on it produces a redirection, and on what the 2029 review does with
the plans submitted. The fuller register the row requires — the
export-control regime, the continuity conditions on connectivity, the
frontier licences, the revenue arrangements, the Intel stake, each set
against the elections since 2024 — NEEDS BASELINE and is not scored
until it exists. *India:* NO BASELINE — not scored. *China:* n/a. The
negative form is Crouch's: "But they had voted for it; the policy had
democratic legitimacy" (PDF p. 14) — an election that legitimates a
commitment it did not choose is the ornamental reading, and a
redirection that is later reversed without an election is scored as
punishment, not remedy.

**DC-2 — Legislatures that enforce meaningful conditions.** Measures
whether a legislature has written conditions onto the stack's
commitments, whether waivers of them are taken and reported, and whether
a condition has been enforced against the executive or a supplier.
*Level:* democratic accountability; the baselines sit at enacted
authority, and the enforcement limb NEEDS BASELINE. *United States:* the
NDAA FY2026 writes conditions of exactly the kind the row requires, each
with a waiver and a reporting duty attached: contractors may not use
"covered artificial intelligence" in performing Department contracts,
with the Secretary's case-by-case waiver (Sec. 1532(a)(3), (b)); a
standardised assessment framework and governance structure for every
artificial-intelligence model the Department employs, under the Chief
Digital and Artificial Intelligence Officer (Sec. 1533(a)); commercial
space systems usable for warfighting and control only under military
direction, with a ten-day notification of any waiver to the defence
committees (Sec. 1603); a specific authorisation request to Congress
before any multiyear contract for a major system (Sec. 804(a)); and the
critical-munitions report (Sec. 364). The GENIUS Act's 1:1 reserve rule
(Sec. 4(a)(1)(A)) is the legislature's condition on the payments layer.
DATED at enacted authority. The demonstrated-compliance limb — waivers
taken under Secs. 1532(b) and 1603(c), notifications made, conditions
enforced — NEEDS BASELINE as a coded series from the Act's enactment.
*Europe:* the co-decided regulations (DMA, DSA, AI Act, eIDAS 2.0) are the
Parliament's conditions on the platform layer, enacted; EDIP is the
Parliament's condition on the fiscal-military layer, at proposal — the
one row in this appendix where the fourth level is a live datum for
Europe, and it moves when the regulation is adopted or refused (COM(2024)
150; DATED at proposal). *India:* NO BASELINE — not scored. *China:* n/a.
The historical portrait of the ornamental outcome is Stasavage's Cortes
of Castile, whose representatives "failed to effectively use even those
formal powers that they did have" and which the monarchy "did away with
… entirely" after 1665 (Stasavage 2011, ch. 7); the general rule, "it
may be possible to maintain the institution but without it serving its
intended purpose" (ch. 8), is the row's coding rule. The positive case is
Glete's Sweden of the early 1620s, where "the crown tried to change the
tax into a permanent obligation to maintain 30,000 soldiers, but that
was denied" (Glete 2002, p. 122): a legislature that enforced, and a
fisc that did not become permanent.

**DC-3 — Courts whose remedies change operations.** Measures whether a
court order altered an operation — a divestiture executed, a directive
enjoined and the operation changed — as distinct from a fine or censure.
*Level:* democratic accountability; the baseline is C11-3's docket
series, re-coded. *United States:* one instance coded as remedy — the
Anthropic injunction of 26 March 2026, after which the GSA restored the
status quo ante on 3 April 2026 (ch11 sources.md; DATED); *FTC v. Meta*
produced no remedy; the Google search judgment produced conduct remedies
and no divestiture (Final Judgment, December 2025; in corpus); the
ad-tech matter awaits a final remedies judgment (in corpus at the
liability opinion and proposed final judgment). *Europe:* the General
Court's dismissal of Apple's designation challenge is a remedy withheld
from the subject, not one imposed on the state (8 July 2026; judgment
number to pin). *India:* NO BASELINE — not scored. *China:* n/a. Coding:
an order the state invited by contract is scored differently from one it
resisted (Krasner 1999, p. 29); the forum-migration limb stays with C11-3
and is not double-counted here.

**DC-4 — Public institutions capable of replacing essential suppliers.**
Measures whether the bloc holds, or has enacted the means to build, a
public substitute for each essential supplier — sovereign compute, a
public foundry stake, a public launch or constellation capacity, a public
identity wallet. *Level:* operating capacity, with the enacted-authority
baselines below; the full inventory NEEDS BASELINE per bloc from 2024.
*United States:* the Intel stake (August 2025; C10-1) as the one public
ownership position in the leading node; the delivery gap (C10-3) as the
capacity proxy; and at the enacted level the Army's programme for
"robotic automation capabilities for munitions manufacturing at
government-owned, contractor-operated production facilities" (NDAA
FY2026, Sec. 225(a)) and the Defense Industrial Base Fund's extended uses
(Sec. 867). DATED. The dated zero is Ruggie's: in 1993 "even the U.S.
Pentagon is baffled by the problem of how to maintain the national
identity of 'its' defense-industrial base" and could not define the unit
of the "reconstitution" model it was considering (Ruggie 1993, pp.
172–73). *Europe:* eIDAS 2.0's wallet obligation on every Member State
(Art. 5a(1)) — the European entry, an obligation to provide, not yet a
wallet provided; EDIP at proposal (€1,500m to 31 December 2027); IRIS²
about 60 per cent public, services c. 2030. DATED at enacted authority.
*China:* the inverse case — the state's own conglomerates as the
suppliers no licence has subordinated (Cheung 2022, p. 115) — entered as
its own thing and scored on three levels. *India:* NO BASELINE — not
scored. The dependence term's anchor is Chastellain's: the duke who broke
every bank in his lands and was then "constraint de les y remettre par
la nécessité publique" (*Œuvres* t. III, p. 316) — coercive superiority
without a substitute for the function — and Glete's, the state left with
"no realistic alternative but to negotiate new contracts with little
competition" (Glete 2002, p. 131).

**DC-5 — The present indicator: the fusion.** Spine §8(g) item 3 names
the present indicator of the ornamental route as the fusion of the barons
with the state against the state's own representative institutions —
absorption by alliance rather than by revocation. *Level:* democratic
accountability; scored on rule 5(b)'s contract coding of the American
absorption events. *United States:* the 2026 contract-coded events are
the row's starting points — the Intel stake taken by agreement (August
2025), the Detachment 201 commissions (13 June 2025), the $1 agency
agreements (August 2025), and the H200 revenue arrangement (CA-6; NEEDS
VERIFICATION) — each DATED; the series the row requires (personnel
flows, contract terms, board seats, immunities granted) NEEDS BASELINE.
*Europe, India:* NO BASELINE — not scored. *China:* n/a. *Direction:*
contract events accumulating while the coercion events of Part V stall is
the ornamental route in progress; revocation exercised against an
incumbent is the democratic route. The predecessors are named: Crouch's
"new dominant, combined political and economic class" (PDF p. 20); Lane's
fourth owner of the protection-selling government, "a dominant class",
under which "a subordinate government would tend to maximize monopoly
profits" (Tilly 1985); Suleyman's firms that "will rub against, capture,
and reengineer the state" (Suleyman 2023).

**DC-6 — The fiscal mode of the consolidator: stack rents or bargained
taxation.** Spine §8(g) item 4 carries the fiscal mechanism into the main
argument: where the consolidating state is financed by stack rents rather
than by bargained broad taxation, Tilly's own direction — that
representation and rights were the by-products of struggle over the means
of war, and that states which "acquired their military organization from
outside, without the same internal forging of mutual constraints between
rulers and ruled … harbor powerful, unconstrained organizations" (Tilly
1985) — predicts consolidation without the by-products. The inference
from external supply to domestic rents is the book's own, marked as such.
*Level:* operating capacity (the fisc's composition); enacted authority
(the instrument). *United States:* the H200 arrangement as contract-income
(NEEDS VERIFICATION at the instrument); the Intel stake as
contract-ownership (DATED); a series of stack-derived receipts against
appropriated revenue NEEDS BASELINE. *China:* the inverse — the state
financing its stack from securitised household savings and guidance funds
at "41 percent of the average official annual defense budget" (Cheung
2022, p. 278), with public-security expenditure at 122.2 per cent of the
defence budget in 2018 (Table 2.1, p. 73; DATED) — the consolidator paying
for the gaze before the gun, a consistency check and not a falsifier.
*Europe:* the fiscal zero of 1.27 per cent (Zielonka 2006, p. 96) and the
EDIP proposal's €1,500m — a bargained instrument, co-decided, at
proposal; DATED. *India:* NO BASELINE — not scored. *Direction:* a
consolidator whose stack finance is rents rather than bargained taxation
is predicted to take the ornamental route; the two historical cases sit
on opposite sides — Glete's Spain, where the state's "own financial
apparatus … had become a new aristocracy" (Glete 2002, pp. 125–26), and
his Sweden, where Karl XI made absolutism "without abolishing the
parliament" and with the peasants' consent (p. 195).

## V. The American sub-wager (CA-1..8)

Kept together per spine §8(f) and scored under rule 3: a failure here is a
failure of the wager. The unifying criterion is the revocation test — does
the state, by 2030, hold reviewable authority over who computes,
communicates, transacts, authenticates or connects, while equity stays
private? Each row is coded under rule 5(b) and (c), and each is read at
the four levels; the American democratic-accountability level rests on
DC-1..3.

**CA-1 — Common-carrier designation of critical cloud.** Statutory or
regulatory designation of a hyperscale cloud provider as a common carrier
or essential service with nondiscrimination, continuity and appeal
duties. *Level:* enacted authority. *Baseline (2026):* none in United
States law (an absence; [RE-CHECK AT PRESS]); the comparator is the CMA's
cloud services market investigation, final decision of 31 July 2025 (in
corpus; DATED). *Threshold:* designation enacted or ordered by end-2029 is
consolidation via carriage, the Interstate Commerce Act route; none, the
sub-wager weakens. *Disconfirmation:* a federal statute expressly
disclaiming common-carrier treatment of cloud. Coded coercion.

**CA-2 — Continuity conditions on strategic connectivity.** Federal
launch, spectrum or defence contracts conditioned on continuity,
lawful-priority and escalation duties, with a documented override.
*Level:* enacted authority; operating capacity. *Baseline:* the
suspension week of March 2025 (C12-1; DATED) at the capacity level; at
the enacted level the NDAA FY2026's Sec. 1603 — commercial space systems
used for warfighting and control only "under the direction of a member
of the armed forces", waivers notified within ten days — which is the
condition the row asked for, in statute, for the Department's own
operations; the contractual form for the operator's civil service remains
to be shown, and the reported shutdown-order investigation is UNVERIFIED
and not carried. *Threshold:* continuity and override conditions written
into contract or statute for the operator by 2028. Coded coercion. The
cleanest revocation-rights test in the set.

**CA-3 — Frontier-compute licensing.** A statutory licence to train above
a compute threshold, with beneficial-ownership disclosure. *Level:*
enacted authority. *Baseline:* the federal reporting regime of EO 14110
rescinded by EO 14148 (20 January 2025; Federal Register; ch11
sources.md; DATED); California SB 53 enacted as the state floor (Chapter
138, Statutes of 2025, chaptered 29 September 2025; in corpus; DATED);
and, inside the Department, the NDAA FY2026's exclusion of covered
artificial intelligence with case-by-case waiver (Sec. 1532) and its
model-assessment framework (Sec. 1533) — review authority over which
models the state and its contractors run, which is licensing's
antechamber and not its statute. *Threshold:* a federal licensing statute
by 2029 is licensing-not-socialisation confirmed (spine §5, §8(c));
executive-only or none is the American route stalled at the
pre-antitrust moment. Coded coercion only where revocation is exercisable
against an incumbent (rule 5(c)); a licence that freezes rivals is
capture-by-licence and scores against the book.

**CA-4 — Structural separation executed, not ordered.** A completed
divestiture severing a platform's vertical link. *Level:* demonstrated
compliance. *Baseline:* the Google ad-tech liability opinion with the
plaintiffs' proposed final judgment and no final remedies judgment at
capture; the Google search Final Judgment (December 2025) stopping short
of divestiture; *FTC v. Meta* lost at trial and on appeal (all in corpus;
ch12 sources.md; DATED). *Threshold:* one executed structural separation
by 2030 is the Paramount/AT&T route live; none is conduct remedies only,
the Microsoft route — consolidation of a weaker kind, scored partial.
Coded coercion.

**CA-5 — A standing digital regulator.** Enactment of a Digital Platform
Commission or equivalent with rulemaking and structural-remedy referral
authority. *Level:* enacted authority. *Baseline:* S. 1671, the Digital
Platform Commission Act of 2023, introduced and not enacted (118th
Congress; in corpus; DATED). *Threshold:* enacted by 2028 is the ICC/SEC
pattern repeating; not enacted by 2030 is the American route relying on
episodic litigation, scored against the thesis. Coded coercion.

**CA-6 — Revenue cut against control right (the H200 test).** Whether the
state's take from controlled-chip exports remains a revenue share
(income) or is replaced by revocation authority (control). *Level:*
enacted authority. *Baseline:* the licence side is DATED — the BIS revised
licence-review policy of December 2025 treating the H200 case by case
(ch10 sources.md), following the H20 licence requirement of April 2025
(Nvidia 8-K) and the rescission of the AI-diffusion rule (BIS, 13 May
2025; in corpus); the revenue side — the reported 15 per cent
arrangement — NEEDS VERIFICATION at the primary instrument and is
carried, not scored, until it lands. *Threshold:* the cut replaced or
subordinated by licensing and revocation terms by 2028 is spine §8(c)'s
predicted correction; the cut entrenched is the venality reading, and a
failure under rule 3. The disconfirmation side is the exit series of
C11-2. This is the sub-wager's sharpest single test; coded contract
until the correction.

**CA-7 — Cross-ownership and interlock enforcement.** Clayton §8 actions
or statutory extension to platform, model, chip and cloud boards and
observer seats. *Level:* demonstrated compliance. *Baseline:* the DOJ's
announcement that seven directors resigned from the boards of five
companies "in response to concerns by the Antitrust Division that their
roles violated the Clayton Act's prohibition on interlocking
directorates", the "first in a broader review" (DOJ press release
22-1222, 19 October 2022; in corpus; DATED). *Threshold:* extension to
non-corporate entities and minority stakes by 2029. Coded coercion.

**CA-8 — Inward-open, outward-closed.** The pairing of domestic
interoperability or portability mandates with export controls on chips,
tools and servicing. *Level:* enacted authority. *Baseline:* the outward
half DATED — the VEU revocation for the Samsung, SK hynix and Intel sites
in China (90 FR, doc. 2025-16735, 2 September 2025; in corpus); the
ASML export stops carried at
T3 (Farrell and Newman 2023, ch. 6) and the servicing denial UNVERIFIED;
the inward half an absence — no federal portability statute ([RE-CHECK AT
PRESS]). *Threshold:* both halves present by 2030 is the state licensing
the guilds at home while embargoing the foundry abroad — the full Tudor
move in the American idiom. Coded coercion.

*Scoring rule for Part V.* CA-6 and CA-2 are the discriminating tests;
CA-1, CA-3 and CA-5 the institutional; CA-4 and CA-7 the structural; CA-8
the composite. The sub-wager WINS if CA-6 turns and any two of CA-1, CA-3
and CA-5 are enacted by 2030; it FAILS if CA-6 entrenches and none of
CA-1, CA-3 and CA-5 is enacted — and under rule 3 that failure is the
wager's. Everything between is scored partial, with the lag in mind: in
1911 wealth and power were decoupled in one act and the public
off-switch was built over a generation, by regulation (ch12). Under rule
5(c), a reconstitution outcome — the severance reversed with federal
approval, as the Bells were reconstituted in 2008 (Wu, ch. 18) — is a
named failure mode, not a partial.
